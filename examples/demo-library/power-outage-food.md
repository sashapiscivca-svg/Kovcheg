# Keeping food safe during a power outage

Summary of the public guidance of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) on food safety during power outages.

## While the power is out

Keep the refrigerator and freezer doors closed as much as possible. An unopened refrigerator keeps food cold for about 4 hours. A full freezer holds its temperature for about 48 hours, a half-full freezer for about 24 hours, if the door stays closed.

## After the power is back

Use an appliance thermometer. The refrigerator should be at 4 °C (40 °F) or below, the freezer at −18 °C (0 °F) or below.

Discard refrigerated perishable food such as meat, poultry, fish, eggs, milk and leftovers that has been above 4 °C (40 °F) for more than 2 hours. Never taste food to decide whether it is safe.

Food in the freezer that still contains ice crystals, or is at 4 °C (40 °F) or below, can be safely refrozen or cooked.
