# Basic emergency supply kit

Summary of the public checklist of Ready.gov (U.S. Federal Emergency Management Agency), "Build A Kit".

## What to pack

- Water: about 4 litres (one gallon) per person per day, for several days, for drinking and sanitation.
- Food: at least a several-day supply of non-perishable food, and a manual can opener.
- A battery-powered or hand-crank radio, a flashlight, and extra batteries.
- A first aid kit and a whistle to signal for help.
- Dust masks, plastic sheeting and duct tape to shelter in place.
- Moist towelettes, garbage bags and plastic ties for personal sanitation.
- A wrench or pliers to turn off utilities.
- Local maps, and a mobile phone with chargers and a backup battery.

## Keeping the kit ready

Keep the kit in a cool, dry place and in containers that are easy to carry. Replace expired items as needed, and rethink your needs every year as your family's situation changes.
